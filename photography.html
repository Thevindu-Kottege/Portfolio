PERSPECTIVE & CRAFT

Observing the quiet beauty in natural light.

Photography is where I explore without commercial constraints. Whether waiting for monsoon clouds to part over Colombo’s brutalist streets or catching the low golden skim of light across southern tides, my approach is quiet, patient, and deeply grounded in observation.

My work as a designer and video editor shapes how I understand balance, visual weight, and frame geometry — but behind the lens, nature and real moments always lead the story.
