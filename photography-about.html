PHOTOGRAPHER & OBSERVER

Observing the world through light, stillness, and patience.

I am a photographer based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. My photographic work is an observational pursuit — an ongoing exploration of natural atmosphere, architectural lines, and the quiet moments between events.

Mirissa Bay, Southern Coast 35mm · f/2.8
CRAFT & METHOD

Restrained Equipment

I believe in moving light and working with available conditions rather than excessive gear. I primarily shoot with full-frame prime lenses (24mm, 35mm, and 85mm), letting the natural ambient light dictate exposure and tone.

Natural Light Prime Optics True Tonalities Unobtrusive Framing
APPROACH & PERSPECTIVE

Finding rhythm in
the unscripted.

Photography began as my personal counterbalance to commercial deadlines. While graphic design and video editing demand precision, keyframes, and strict client guidelines, photography is fundamentally an act of receptive looking. You cannot hurry morning fog over a tea plantation, nor can you negotiate with the angle of the sun as it touches the Indian Ocean.

Places & Atmospheric Conditions

My work focuses predominantly on landscapes, coastal horizons, and urban architecture. I am drawn to transitional states: the stillness of streets immediately following tropical rain, the stark shadows cast by concrete facades at midday, and the delicate gradients of coastal twilight. Sri Lanka’s varied geography — from humid coastlines to cold central ridges — offers an endless canvas for tonal exploration.

The Intersect of Design and Lens

My background as a graphic designer influences how I compose each frame: looking for negative space, geometric alignment, and textural harmony. Similarly, my experience as a video editor makes me sensitive to rhythm and narrative sequences — viewing photographs not just as isolated frames, but as chapters within a larger visual essay.
